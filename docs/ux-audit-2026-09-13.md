## Summary

The site is fast, clean, and the resume is one click away on desktop. The biggest problem is content, not layout. The page stops at Uniblox (Jun 2025), while the new resume leads with a current PM role at Volt AI, and several titles and dates disagree between the two. A recruiter who cross-checks will notice. The second problem is that the first screen never says what role Akhil wants.

## Findings

### Critical (blocks user task)

None.

### High (causes confusion or friction)

- **Site contradicts the new resume, and the current role is missing**: The Experience Timeline starts at Uniblox (Apr to Jun 2025). The resume leads with Volt AI, Product Management, May 2026 to present. Other mismatches a recruiter could spot:
  - Govt of Andhra Pradesh starts Sep 2017 on the site, Sep 2018 on the resume.
  - CGAP title is "Co-founder & Director" on the site, "Cofounder and Head of Projects" on the resume.
  - Uniblox is "Pre-MBA Product Intern" in Featured Work but "Pre-MBA Product Management" in the timeline, on the same page.
  - The Andhra Pradesh card says "3 tech vendors, 2 consulting firms". The resume says "5 tech partners".
  - The Uniblox card says "reducing compute costs". The resume has the number: 85% less infrastructure cost.
  - *Where*: `#experience`, `#work`
  - *Fix*: Update `client/src/data/experience.ts` and `client/src/data/projects.ts` from the resume, then `npm run generate:llms`. Decide which date is right for Andhra Pradesh.

- **The first screen doesn't say what he's looking for**: The hero covers the think tank and "scaling impact through technology". It never mentions PM or PMM, the Volt AI role, or the May 2027 graduation. `llms.txt` says "pursuing product management and product marketing roles", but people never see that file. The recruiter has to infer fit from the timeline.
  - *Where*: hero, `client/src/data/bio.ts`
  - *Screenshot*: `01-desktop-above-fold.png`
  - *Fix*: Add one line above the bio, e.g. "PM at Volt AI this summer. Haas MBA '27, looking for full-time product roles." The hero bio was restored on purpose in a recent commit, so this is Akhil's call.

- **Side project cards can't be reached with a keyboard**: Each card is a `<Card onClick>` div with no role, `tabIndex`, or key handler (`client/src/components/projects-section.tsx:19`). Tab jumps from "Scroll to featured work" straight past all five cards. The case studies behind them, with the live demo and GitHub links, are unreachable without a mouse. When the modal closes, focus falls to `<body>`.
  - *Where*: `#projects`
  - *Fix*: Render the card as a `<button>` (or add `role="button"`, `tabIndex={0}`, and Enter/Space handling). Radix Dialog will then return focus to the card on close.

### Medium (suboptimal but workable)

- **Nothing signals that project cards open a case study**: No arrow, no "Read more", only a hover lift that touch screens never show. The richest content on the site (CalEvents, MirrorMe with live links) is easy to skip.
  - *Screenshot*: `04-desktop-SideProjects.png`, `16-desktop-after-card-click.png`
  - *Fix*: Add a "Case study" label or the same arrow icon Featured Work uses.

- **On a phone, the first screen is only the photo**: At 375 px the portrait fills the viewport. Name, bio and CTA start below the fold. The resume sits in the hamburger menu or about 10,000 px down the page.
  - *Screenshot*: `02-mobile-above-fold.png`
  - *Fix*: Put name and tagline above the photo on small screens, or shrink the photo. Consider a Resume button in the hero.

- **Unknown URLs show Vercel's raw 404**: `/doesnotexist` (or a guess like `/resume`) returns plain text "The page could not be found / NOT_FOUND / sfo1::..." with no way back. The app's own `NotFound` page never renders, and its copy says "Did you forget to add the page to the router?" (`client/src/pages/not-found.tsx:15`).
  - *Fix*: Add a static `client/public/404.html` with a link home and to the resume. That keeps a real 404 status. Optionally rewrite `/resume` to the PDF in `vercel.json`.

- **Touch targets under 44 px on mobile**: theme toggle and menu (36 px), external link arrows (20 to 24 px), contact links (20 px tall), "View projects" (23 px).
  - *Fix*: Pad these to at least 44 by 44.

- **Buttons nested inside links**: Social icons and the modal's demo/GitHub links wrap a `<Button>` inside an `<a>` (`client/src/components/social-links.tsx:22`, `projects-section.tsx:73`). Invalid HTML. Each icon takes two tab stops, and the second one is an unnamed "button".
  - *Fix*: Use `<Button asChild><a ...></a></Button>`.

### Low (polish)

- **Back leaves the site**: Nav clicks change the hash without adding history entries, so Back after jumping between sections exits the portfolio.
- **Copy inconsistencies**: "customisation" (hero, Andhra Pradesh entry) vs "customization" (elsewhere). "Scaling Impact through technology" capitalises only one word. "growing 140+ members" is missing "to". "Steering Committee/ Advisor" has a stray space. "Gender And Politics" capitalises "And".
- **Three `<h1>` elements**: sidebar name, mobile header name, and hero. Only the hero should be a heading.
- **`www` doesn't redirect**: `www.akhilneelam.com` serves a 200 copy. The canonical tag limits the SEO damage. Set www to redirect to the apex in Vercel domains.
- **Dark mode loses emphasis**: the Resume button drops its pink fill, and timeline dots fade to near-invisible (`26-desktop-dark-hero.png`, `27-desktop-dark-experience.png`). The site also ignores the system theme (`client/src/App.tsx:20` forces light).
- **Overlapping labels**: Nav has "Work" and "Projects". The Personal section repeats a "Side Projects" card whose button scrolls back up to Projects.
- **Mobile timeline metadata misaligned**: date sits left, location sits indented right (`23-mobile-experience-after-scroll.png`).

## Workflow Comprehension

### Threads Tested
- **Screen and download**: land, skim hero, click Resume.
  - Completable end-to-end? Yes. One click on desktop, two taps on mobile.
  - Where the user gets lost: working out target role from the hero.
- **Check depth of work**: Work, then Projects, then open a case study.
  - Completable end-to-end? Yes with a mouse. No with a keyboard.
  - Missing connections: Featured Work items (except CGAP) have no link or detail.
- **Reach out**: Contact section or sidebar email icon.
  - Completable end-to-end? Yes. Email, LinkedIn and GitHub are all one click.

### Wayfinding
- Can the user always tell where they are? Yes. The sidebar highlights the section in view.
- Do nav labels match user language? Mostly. "Work" vs "Projects" needs a second look.
- Deep links: `/#experience` loads scrolled to the right section on mobile and desktop.

### Visual Cues
- The eye goes to the name and photo first, then the quiet "View Work" button. The Resume button is the strongest CTA but sits in the sidebar corner.

"It's one long page. The left sidebar jumps to each section. Work is his main jobs, Projects are side builds, and clicking a project card opens the full story with a live link. Resume download is the pink button at the bottom of the sidebar, or in the menu on your phone."

## Checked and Cleared

- Mobile menu: opens, closes on selection, scrolls to the chosen section, includes Download Resume.
- Scroll reveal (`fade-in-view`): all content appears during normal scrolling. The only unrevealed items are carousel slides still off screen. Blank frames in early mobile screenshots came from instant programmatic jumps.
- No horizontal overflow at 375, 768, 1024 or 1280 px.
- No console errors or failed requests on desktop or mobile.

## What Works Well

- Fast: TTFB 9 ms, FCP 284 ms, LCP 1.08 s (AVIF hero image), CLS 0.
- Resume is always one click away on desktop, and the Contact card says plainly "Looking for my resume? Download the latest version here."
- Project case studies are well written. They start with a real problem and end with what he learned.
- The sticky sidebar with section highlighting makes a long page easy to move around.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the fade animations.
- Theme toggle works and remembers the choice.

4. Fix the mobile first screen so the name and a Resume button show without scrolling.
